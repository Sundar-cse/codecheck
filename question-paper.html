GENERAL RULES

  1. This challenge has two rounds: Round 1 (Easy) and Round 2 (Hard).
  2. Each participant must select one language from C, C++, Python, or Java and use it for both rounds.
  3. Each round contains one debugging question. The buggy program is provided on the official BUG-404 website.
  4. Correct the program, execute it on the official website, and submit your final code before the round closes.
  5. Round 1 carries 10 marks and Round 2 carries 20 marks. Maximum score: 30 marks.
  6. Each round has its own timer. Round 1: 5 minutes. Round 2: 10 minutes.
  7. Do not copy code, share answers, or communicate with other participants during the challenge.
  8. Use the official website only for code execution and submission. Do not refresh the page unnecessarily.
  9. Marks are based on program correctness. Organizers may review submitted code and award partial credit for a valid correction or approach.
  10. Follow instructions from event coordinators. The organizers' decision on evaluation and results is final.
